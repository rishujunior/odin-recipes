Mutton biryani

Description

Mutton biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with tender pieces of mutton, basmati rice, aromatic spices, and herbs. The mutton is cooked with a spiced mixture and layered with partially cooked rice before being cooked together until the flavors are well combined.

It is commonly served with hot with raita, salads and along with other side dishes.

Ingredients

  • 500 g mutton
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2-3 green chilies
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • 2-3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Biryani spices such as cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and bay leaf

Steps

  1. Wash the mutton and cut it into suitable pieces.
  2. Marinate the mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt, and spices.
  3. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20-30 minutes.
  4. Fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  5. Cook the marinated mutton with tomatoes, green chilies, and spices until tender.
  6. Cook the soaked rice separately until it is partially cooked.
  7. Layer the partially cooked rice over the cooked mutton.
  8. Add mint, coriander, and some fried onions between the layers.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is completely cooked.
  10. Gently mix the layers and serve the biryani hot.
