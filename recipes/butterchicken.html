Butter chicken

Description

Butter chicken, also known as Murgh Makhani, is a popular Indian dish made with tender chicken cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based sauce. The sauce is flavored with butter, aromatic spices, and cream, giving the dish its characteristic smooth and rich taste.

It is commonly served with naan or basmati rice and garnished with fresh coriander.

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 teaspoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 teaspoons red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi
  • 2 teaspoons mustard oil
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2-3 green chilies
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • Whole spices such as cloves, cinnamon, and cardamom
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Place the chicken in a bowl and add yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, salt, lemon juice, garam masala, kasuri methi, and mustard oil.
  2. Mix everything thoroughly and allow the chicken to marinate in the refrigerator for at least one hour.
  3. Roast or grill the marinated chicken until it is browned and mostly cooked.
  4. Heat oil and butter in a pan. Add cloves, cinnamon, and cardamom, and cook briefly until fragrant.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes, ginger, and garlic. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  6. Blend the cooked tomato mixture into a smooth puree.
  7. Heat some butter in the pan and add the tomato puree along with red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and kasuri methi.
  8. Add the roasted chicken pieces to the gravy and simmer until the chicken is completely cooked and the flavors combine.
  9. Add fresh cream and mix gently. Simmer for a few minutes.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan or basmati rice.
